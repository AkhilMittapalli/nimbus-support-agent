# Support Channels and Service Level Agreement

## Support channels by tier

- **Free:** community forum; best-effort email within 72 hours.
- **Starter:** email support during business hours; first response within 24 hours.
- **Pro:** email and chat, 24 hours on weekdays; first response within 8 hours; urgent issues within 4 hours.
- **Enterprise:** 24/7 phone, chat and email; named Technical Account Manager; urgent issues within 1 hour.

## Priority definitions

- **Urgent:** production outage or data loss affecting many users, or a security incident.
- **High:** a core feature is broken with no workaround.
- **Medium:** a feature is degraded or a workaround exists; billing disputes.
- **Low:** questions, how-to requests and feature requests.

## Uptime SLA and service credits

The SLA applies to Pro (99.9% monthly uptime) and Enterprise (99.95% monthly uptime) only. When monthly uptime falls below the commitment, customers may claim service credits:

| Monthly uptime | Pro credit | Enterprise credit |
|----------------|-----------|-------------------|
| Below commitment but at least 99.0% | 10% of monthly fee | 15% of monthly fee |
| 95.0% to 99.0% | 25% of monthly fee | 30% of monthly fee |
| Below 95.0% | 50% of monthly fee | 50% of monthly fee |

SLA credit claims must be submitted within 30 days of the incident and are processed by the Billing team through an escalation ticket. Credits are applied to the next invoice and are not paid out in cash.
