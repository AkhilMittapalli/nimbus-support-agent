# Account Security and Identity Verification

## Verifying identity with support

Before discussing account-specific details, making changes or issuing refunds, support must verify the customer's identity. Customers verify with the email address on the account and their 4-digit support PIN, which is shown in the Admin Console under Profile > Security. After 3 failed verification attempts in one session, support must stop trying and escalate to the Security team with priority high.

## Password resets

Support agents never reset passwords or ask for them. Customers reset their password from the sign-in page by choosing "Forgot password". The reset link expires after 30 minutes. If the reset email does not arrive, check spam folders and confirm the email address is correct.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

2FA can be enabled with an authenticator app or a hardware security key. If a customer loses access to their 2FA device, they can sign in with one of their 10 backup codes. If backup codes are also lost, account recovery requires an escalation to the Security team, which verifies ownership through a video call or notarised form. Recovery takes 3 to 5 business days.

## Suspicious activity

If a customer reports unrecognised sign-ins or suspects a compromised account, they should immediately change their password and revoke active sessions in Profile > Security > Sessions. Support must create an urgent escalation ticket for the Security team.
