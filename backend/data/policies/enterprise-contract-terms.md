# Enterprise Contract Terms and Account Management

## Contract structure

Enterprise customers sign an annual order form with a committed seat count. Additional seats can be added at any time at the contracted per-seat rate and are prorated to the contract end date. Seat counts cannot be reduced until renewal.

## Renewals

Contracts renew automatically for 12 months unless either party gives written notice at least 60 days before the contract end date. Price changes at renewal are capped at 7%.

## Technical Account Manager (TAM)

Every Enterprise account has a named TAM who handles quarterly business reviews, contract changes, early termination requests and escalations that need executive involvement.

## Data residency

Enterprise customers choose a data region (US, EU or APAC) at onboarding. Migrating data between regions is a professional services engagement and must be requested through the TAM.

## Enterprise refunds and credits

Enterprise refunds follow the Refund Policy: up to $1,000 per refund within 60 days of the invoice date when the contract is active. Larger amounts or contract-level credits require Billing team approval through an escalation ticket.
