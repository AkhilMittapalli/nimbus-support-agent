# Billing, Invoices and Payment Methods

## Billing cycle

Starter and Pro plans are billed monthly in advance on the anniversary of the sign-up date. Enterprise contracts are billed annually or monthly according to the signed order form.

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed to the account's billing email address and are available in the Admin Console under Billing > Invoices. Each invoice has an ID in the format `INV-` followed by four digits. Customers can change their billing email address through support after verifying their identity, or in the Admin Console.

## Payment methods

Starter and Pro accept major credit and debit cards. Enterprise customers may also pay by wire transfer or ACH with net-30 terms.

## Failed payments and past-due accounts

If a payment fails, Nimbus Cloud retries the charge after 3, 5 and 7 days. After the final failed retry the account is marked **past due**. Past-due accounts keep full access for 14 days, then become read-only (status **suspended**). While an account is past due or suspended:

- Refunds cannot be issued by support agents.
- Plan changes are blocked.
- The customer should update their payment method; the outstanding balance is charged automatically.

Questions about disputed past-due balances must be escalated to the Billing team.

## Taxes

Sales tax or VAT is calculated from the billing address. Customers with tax-exempt status must send a valid exemption certificate to the Billing team via an escalation ticket.
