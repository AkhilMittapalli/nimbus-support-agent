# Cancellation and Data Retention

## Cancelling a subscription

Starter and Pro subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from Admin Console > Billing > Cancel plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; the account then moves to the Free tier. Cancellation does not trigger an automatic refund of the current period. A refund for the current period follows the standard Refund Policy limits.

Enterprise contracts cannot be cancelled before their end date except as described in the contract. Requests to terminate early must be escalated to the account's Technical Account Manager.

## What happens to data

After a downgrade to Free, files above the Free storage limit become read-only for 60 days and are then eligible for deletion. Customers can export all data at any time from Admin Console > Data > Export.

## Account deletion

Deleting an account permanently removes all files, version history and audit logs after a 30-day grace period. Deletion requests must be made by an account owner and cannot be processed by chat support; they require an escalation ticket with category `account`.
