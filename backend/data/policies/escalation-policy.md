# Escalation to Human Support

The AI assistant hands a conversation to human support whenever it cannot resolve the request safely. Every escalation creates a ticket with a category, a priority and a summary so that the customer does not have to repeat themselves.

## When to escalate

- The customer asks for something the assistant is not allowed to do, such as refunds above the tier limit, account deletion or early contract termination.
- The knowledge base has no reliable answer to a support question.
- Identity verification fails 3 times.
- The customer reports a security incident or data loss.
- The customer explicitly asks for a human.
- A system the assistant depends on is unavailable.

## Categories

`billing`, `technical`, `security`, `account`, `contracts` and `general`.

## What the customer should expect

The customer receives the ticket ID. Response times follow the support SLA for their tier. Urgent security tickets are handled by the on-call Security team at any hour for every tier.
