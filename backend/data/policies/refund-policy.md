# Refund Policy

Nimbus Cloud offers refunds on paid invoices when a customer is unhappy with a charge, was billed in error, or downgraded mid-cycle. Refund eligibility depends on the customer's subscription tier, the age of the invoice, and the status of the customer's contract.

## Eligibility requirements

A refund can only be issued when all of the following are true:

- The customer's identity has been verified in the current support session.
- The customer's contract status is **active**. Accounts that are past due, suspended, or cancelled are not eligible for agent-issued refunds; these must be escalated to the Billing team.
- The invoice belongs to the verified customer and still has a refundable balance.
- The refund is requested within the refund window for the customer's tier.
- The refund amount does not exceed the per-refund limit for the customer's tier.

## Refund limits by tier

| Tier | Per-refund limit | Refund window (days from invoice date) |
|------|------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| Free | Not applicable (no paid invoices) | Not applicable |
| Starter | $50.00 | 14 days |
| Pro | $200.00 | 30 days |
| Enterprise | $1,000.00 | 60 days |

Partial refunds are allowed. The amount refunded can never exceed the remaining refundable balance on the invoice.

## Requests above the limit or outside the window

Support agents cannot override tier limits. If a customer requests more than their tier's per-refund limit, or the invoice is older than the refund window, the agent must create an escalation ticket for the Billing team with category `billing`. Billing reviews these within 2 business days and may approve exceptions, for example for service outages covered by the SLA.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Card refunds typically appear within 5 to 10 business days. Invoice-based (wire transfer) Enterprise customers receive a credit note within 3 business days.

## Non-refundable items

- Professional services hours that have already been delivered.
- Domain registration fees.
- Usage-based overage charges older than 7 days.
