# Subscription Tiers and Plan Features

Nimbus Cloud is a file sync, storage and team collaboration platform. It is sold in four tiers.

## Free

- 5 GB storage per user, up to 3 users.
- 30-day file version history.
- Community forum support only; email responses are best effort within 72 hours.
- No SLA.

## Starter ($29 per month)

- 100 GB storage per user, up to 10 users.
- 90-day file version history.
- Email support, business hours (Mon to Fri, 9am to 6pm in the customer's region), first response within 24 hours.
- Shared links with passwords and expiry dates.

## Pro ($149 per month)

- 1 TB storage per user, unlimited users.
- 180-day version history and file recovery.
- Email and chat support, 24 hours on weekdays, first response within 8 hours.
- Admin console, SSO with Google Workspace, audit logs (90 days).
- 99.9% uptime SLA with service credits.

## Enterprise (custom annual contract)

- Unlimited storage (fair use), unlimited users.
- 1-year version history, legal hold and eDiscovery export.
- 24/7 phone, chat and email support with a named Technical Account Manager, first response within 1 hour for urgent issues.
- SAML SSO with any identity provider, SCIM provisioning, data residency options (US, EU, APAC).
- 99.95% uptime SLA with service credits.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period; storage over the new tier's limit becomes read-only until usage is reduced. Enterprise contracts can only be changed by the account's Technical Account Manager.
